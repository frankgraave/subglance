Incidents

Everything here is backed by endpoints that already exist: GET /api/v1/incidents, GET /api/v1/monitors/{id}/incidents, POST /api/v1/incidents/{id}/ack. The blue and amber blocks on this page are review annotations, not product UI.

Why the detail expands inline instead of filling a right-hand panel. A .split side panel is 320px, which is too narrow for an error string, a timeline and a captured response body without wrapping all three into mush — and it makes the whole page reflow the moment you click a row at 3am. Expanding in place keeps the incident where your eye already is, keeps the rows above and below in context, and lets the snapshot use the full content width. Multiple rows can be open at once, which is how you compare two failures that started a minute apart.
Question 1 — chronological or grouped by monitor? This list is strictly newest first. When one monitor flaps, that is right (one incident, flaps suppressed), but when a shared dependency takes out four monitors at once you get four rows that are really one event. Do we want an optional "group by monitor", or even a heuristic "4 monitors failed within 60s" cluster row — or is grouping a lie we should not tell?
Question 2 — does Acknowledge silence notifications? Right now it is drawn as a pure human marker: "someone is on this", visible to the team, notifications keep firing. The alternative is that ack also stops re-alerts and escalation for this incident until it resolves. That is a much bigger promise and needs to be obvious in the button label ("Acknowledge" vs "Acknowledge & mute"). Which one is SubGlance?

Question 3 — how long do we keep history, and is 30 days the right window? The history section is capped at 30 days here because that is what fits a "grouped by day" list before it needs pagination. But incidents are cheap rows and the SLA question people actually ask is "how did this look last quarter". Do we keep forever and add a date range picker, prune on a retention setting, or keep the page at 30 days and push longer windows into an export?
