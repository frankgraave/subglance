Page proposals

The sidebar promises four destinations that do not exist yet. These are proposals for what they should be. Nothing is wired to a backend — the data is fixed and the interactions are simulated, so it is safe to click anything.

How to read the annotations. Blue notes explain why a layout is the way it is. Amber notes are open questions: decisions I did not want to make on your behalf. Answering those is the useful part of this review — the pixels can move later, the questions shape what gets built.
Status, 16 September. These proposals are no longer only proposals: each page now has a ticket, and Incidents is being built. The annotations were left in place rather than stripped on merge, because the reasoning is the part worth keeping — but an answered question carries its answer in blue, so nobody re-opens a settled decision.

Incidents has all three of its questions answered (both chronological and clustered; ack does silence re-alerts, which contradicts what that page originally drew; history stays at 30 days). Monitors, Notifications and Settings still carry ten amber questions between them. Those are recorded in their tickets, so answering them here or there amounts to the same thing — but nothing is blocked on them except the parts they describe.
